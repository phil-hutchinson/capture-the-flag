# Peer Review — Story 43: Implement the 10 × 10 Board (`CLASH`)

## Summary

The branch publishes `2-0:CLASH` — `asymmetric_100` (10 × 10, lakes at A, D and
G–I) plus `standard_clash` (20 pieces over ranks 1–5) — and graduates both flag
values out of `proposed-variants.md` into `rules.md` Appendix A. The code change
is exactly the four files story §5 predicted (`board.py`, `pieces.py`,
`record.py`, `placement.py`) with no new parameter threaded anywhere, which is
the strongest evidence that story 37's seams held; test coverage is thorough and
the squeeze guard is genuinely improved by the addition of a negative case that
proves the check can fail. All eleven plan steps are accounted for, including the
`README.md` verification step (Step 11), so no comment is raised on that.

**Static checks and tests, run from the repository root:** `pyright` reports
**0 errors, 0 warnings, 0 informations**; `ruff check .` reports **All checks
passed**; `pytest` reports **476 passed, 16 deselected** (the deselected 16 are
the `slow` marker excluded by `addopts`). The reviewer also re-ran the plan's
manual Step 9 verification: `game_runner --ruleset clash` renders a 10-wide A–J
board with lakes at A, D and G–I across rows 5–6 and reaches a legal end;
`batch_runner --ruleset clash` writes records stamped `[Ruleset "2-0:CLASH"]`
with a 10-column position block; and a hand-written 3 × 10 placement file parses
and roster-checks correctly for both sides.

Every finding below is documentation. The recurring one is that the branch
correctly de-two-ified `rules.md` and `technical-notes.md`'s headline passages
but did not sweep the rest of the repository for the same phrasing — which is
precisely the hazard `doc/ruleset/CLAUDE.md` gained a new paragraph about *in
this branch* ("When adding a ruleset, grep for the ones that say *both*").

  §7 (changelog) and the `Documents to change` table has a corresponding change,
  and nothing outside those tables was touched. The plan's Steps 9 and 10 have no
  commits, which is correct — both are verification-only. Step 9 was re-run
  during this review and passes.
- **`README.md` verification step.** Plan Step 11 exists and expects a change
  rather than a confirmation, which is the right framing given three facts in
  `README.md` went stale.
- **The squeeze guard.** `test_the_squeeze_check_detects_one_when_it_exists`
  exceeds what plan Step 4 asked for (a throwaway scratch check) by making the
  guard's own fallibility permanent, and correctly builds the hypothetical from a
  raw lake set rather than a `BoardLayout` so it is not testing the type's
  guarantee against itself. The A1/B2 diagonal is the minimal case and both
  directions are asserted.
- **`test_the_clash_tower_walk_never_stalls` covers only `Side.WHITE`.** This
  matches `test_the_skirmish_tower_walk_never_stalls` exactly, and both sides are
  covered by the parametrised
  `test_random_clash_placement_fills_its_own_home_zone_and_roster`.
- **`README.md`'s "one Skirmish-only placement restriction".** Still accurate —
  `spacing_and_lanes` is set by exactly one edition — even though `rules.md` §3
  now frames the restriction geometrically. Comment 1 covers the framing.
- **No Clash coverage in the neural-network tests.** Out of scope per story
  §"Out of scope" (training a Clash network); `TensorLayout.for_setup` was
  verified by hand to resolve Clash to a distinct 10 × 10 contract.
